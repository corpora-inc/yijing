Yìjīng — Private I Ching Oracle

Make space for a new perspective with the Book of Changes.

Consult the I Ching, explore all 64 hexagrams, and return to past readings in your private journal. Read Chinese text alongside English, Spanish, and pinyin, choosing the languages you want to see together.

Designed for quiet reflection:
• Cast a reading with the traditional three-coin probabilities.
• Read your hexagram, changing lines, and transformed hexagram.
• Explore the hexagrams through the trigram matrix or numbered list.
• Keep questions and reading history on your device.
• Use the app offline, with no account, advertising, or tracking.

External website and support links open only when you choose them.
